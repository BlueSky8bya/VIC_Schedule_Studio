`radio/provenance.json` records every original title, author, source page, observed download URL, original SHA-256 and delivered SHA-256. The delivered music is Opus 64 kb/s, 48 kHz stereo, normalized to -20 LUFS / -2 dBTP with a brief leading fade. Reaction BPM values are pacing hints, not measured beat synchronization. Friendly Korean display titles belong to this project's radio UI; original titles remain in the receipt.

The cushion sample is normalized separately to -25 LUFS / -4 dBTP and encoded as Opus 48 kb/s. Its receipt and the publisher's original `KENNEY-LICENSE.txt` accompany the file. The first click has an immediate synthesized fallback while this small sample becomes available for later clicks.

No YouTube recordings, subscription libraries or Musopen uploads are bundled. No blanket claim is made about platform Content ID or third-party rights outside these stated releases. Future additions require the same source-and-recording license check.
