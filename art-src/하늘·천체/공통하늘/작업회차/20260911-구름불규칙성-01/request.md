## Exact built-in imagegen prompt

+Create ONE production pixel-art CLOUD SPRITE ATLAS with 5 columns and 6 rows, 30 isolated cloud patches, on perfectly flat opaque pure MAGENTA #FF00FF. Use attached image as PALETTE / PIXEL BRUSHWORK reference AND a REJECTED GEOMETRY example. Its regimented identical cloudlets, feather-comb stripes, centered pyramids, equal lobe beads and symmetric anvils are precisely what MUST change. Keep creamy white and muted blue-gray pixel colors and gentle cozy-game feel. Fine visible discrete pixel clusters, 6–10 related shades per cloud, no black outline, no smooth vector beads, no airbrushed gradients.
PRIMARY CHANGE: clouds look naturally formed, asymmetric, broken, wind-torn and irregular at several scales. Each patch must have an off-center main mass, unequal lobes, unequal spacing and thickness, a few uneven gaps, and a tapering ragged edge. Fine cloudlets form irregular loose clumps with large empty gaps, NEVER a grid, diagonal rows, dots, cross-shaped sparkle icons, fish scales or a lattice. Wisps curve in uneven torn streamers, NEVER straight parallel feathers or comb teeth. Layered clouds are skewed ragged fragments, NEVER centered oval disks. Cumulus: a flat-ish uneven base is okay, but asymmetric rising masses, low fragments and uneven cavities, NEVER a centered triangular mountain of equal circles. Cumulonimbus: keep meteorological anvil identity, but wind spreads one side farther, trunk offset, broken lower shelf; NEVER an hourglass, mushroom icon, symmetric bow tie or identical tower.
EXACT layout: 5 columns, 6 rows, no grid lines or labels. Every patch fits inside its cell with generous at least 15% completely blank magenta margins, including detached wisps. Leave clear continuous blank gutters separating all rows and columns. No overlapping neighboring cells. Rows 1–3: column 1 CIRRUS; column 2 CIRROCUMULUS; column 3 CIRROSTRATUS; column 4 ALTOCUMULUS; column 5 ALTOSTRATUS. Rows 4–6: column 1 STRATUS; column 2 STRATOCUMULUS; column 3 NIMBOSTRATUS; column 4 CUMULUS; column 5 CUMULONIMBUS. Three DISTINCT drawn silhouettes per genus, not mirrored or resized copies. Within each type, vary where bulk is placed, widths of gaps, whether silhouette is narrow/wide, fragmented/connected, low/tall. Do not make all clouds point the same way. Nimbostratus is layered gray rain cloud, no separate rain streaks. Cirrostratus is thin irregular ice veil, altostratus broader denser sheet. The sprite grid is orderly only for extraction; the cloud forms INSIDE it must be strikingly organic and non-repeating. No text, no sun/moon/stars, no lightning, no landscape, no checkerboard. Square atlas.
