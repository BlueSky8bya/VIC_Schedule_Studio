# 136 실제 생성 요청

builtin `image_gen.imagegen`, TV/중립 방 서로 다른 자산 각1회. 사용자 첨부 방 참조와135 빈 방 편집 대상을 직접 열어 확인하고 전달했다. 캐릭터 생성 없음. 원본 출력 바이트를 별도136 회차에 복사하고 기존 원본은 유지했다.

## 입체 TV (transparent_background=true)

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Produce ONE isolated illustrated television asset, inspired precisely by the small orange retro television in the LEFT room of the attached owner-approved illustration. The television is a genuinely volumetric rounded cabinet, physically yawed about 30 degrees to its LEFT toward a sofa located off canvas on the viewer's left. The FRONT FACE and SCREEN must point left; show a substantial RIGHT SIDE PANEL with real cabinet depth, a visible top surface, matching vanishing lines and consistent short tapered wooden feet. This is a three-quarter view of the entire thick object, NOT a frontal television skewed as a flat card and NOT only a distorted screen. Keep the reference's hand-painted cozy game illustration, honey orange body, golden cream edges, warm delicate brown contours, soft occlusion, two cream circular knobs on the front right, and a simple small aerial if consistent with the reference. The body should read as the same furniture family and material as the reference, not a glossy photorealistic plastic render or a new toy design. SCREEN OFF: a plain dark charcoal softly curved CRT screen with no scene, reflection graphics or text. Leave the complete screen face clearly visible for a later channel overlay. Camera: mild view from above, with object resting on an unseen horizontal floor; physically consistent three-dimensional form. One television only centered in a nearly square canvas, the full cabinet and feet visible, tight clean transparent alpha background with minimal soft contact shadow only. No room, sofa, floor, window, lamp, other props, people, characters, labels, text, diagram, split panels, or watermark.
```
