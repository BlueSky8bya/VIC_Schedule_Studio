# 135 실제 생성 프롬프트

builtin `image_gen.imagegen`. 두 요청 모두 승인 참조 basename `ChatGPT 이미지 2026년 10월 19일 오후 04_10_19.png`, SHA256 `37bf98a2d1b898dfe3930229b14e719fa83822ff21fbba8e02d11f4afe11c245`를 `referenced_image_paths` 입력으로 전달했다. 이 기록은 프롬프트 원문과 입력 파일의 동일성을 보존하며 사용자 절대 경로는 생략한다. 각 자산1회, 총2회. 캐릭터 생성·자동 재생성 없음.

## 빈 방 — transparent_background=false

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Create ONE empty room background asset for a tiny illustrated companion room, using ONLY the left daytime panel of the attached reference as the approved material, camera and lighting reference. Output a single wide 3:2 image, no split panels. Keep exactly this warm hand-painted cozy game illustration: cream plaster back wall, honey oak horizontal floor boards, very gentle brown edges, soft contact occlusion along the baseboard, golden afternoon light from the upper left, calm dimensional shading, no photorealism. The scene is a shallow front-facing room with only a small sliver of left side wall. The back-wall to floor seam must be at 60 percent of image height, leaving 40 percent floor foreground, so separate real furniture can stand at the seam and in front. Remove EVERY object from the reference: no window, no curtains, no curtain rod, no shelf, no radio, no book, no dispenser, no toy box, no sofa or rug, no TV, no lamp or cord, no trash can, no wash pad, no plants, no characters. Leave the entire back wall plain cream and all the floor empty. The window, furniture and lighting controls will be placed as separate interactive runtime layers, so do not bake any object or object-shaped shadow into this background. Subtle broad light falloff from the unseen upper-left window is okay, but no bright hard rectangle or graphic beam. Canvas edges flush with the room, no outer frame, no text, no labels, no UI, no transparency. Preserve the reference's illustrated warm wood/cream material quality and soft dimensional shadows without introducing new decoration.
```

결과 원본: `../원본/빈방_벽바닥.png`. 원본에는 비스듬한 낮 빛 자국이 남아 오른쪽 중립 재질 구역만 직사각 crop해 사용했다. 재생성하지 않았다.

Preserve the approved mat's soft low oval perspective, cream plush scalloped rim, sage green inner surface, warm thin brown outline, gentle dimensional fabric shading and subtle contact shadow. Remove the wastebasket, TV, floor, room, furniture and all other objects entirely. No brush, no toothbrush stand, no characters, no text, no sparkle, no water, no decorative additions. A single empty mat seen from the same shallow slightly elevated front view as the reference, centered tightly in a wide landscape image, its complete outer silhouette visible. Actual transparent alpha background outside the mat and its minimal soft contact shadow. Keep the rim clean and readable at a final display width of about 58 pixels. This is the distinct wash-mat asset, not a room or mockup.
