# Poster capture rules

- In-app PNG/clipboard export and stickers are retired. Do not restore them from historical instructions.
- When poster screenshots are required for QA, capture the public surface using Playwright and `[data-export-surface]`.
- Poster mode must not render Studio navigation, private-layer badges, edit controls, or unlock controls.
- Current visual contracts live in `docs/ux/UI_RULES.md` and `docs/agent/DEFINITION_OF_DONE.md`.
