# Current ambient engine rules

Updated: 2026-09-11. Scope: scene engine, world services, camera, creatures and ambient control wiring. [AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md) supplies global boundaries; [README](README.md) routes to the specific visual grammar.

## P0 depth amendment — ADR-0022

Meadow now follows [ADR-0024](../agent/decisions/ADR-0024-spring-meadow-three-layers.md) and the authorized [ROUND-22 seasonal expansion](rounds/ROUND-22-meadow-seasons.md): three generated F/M/N sources per season, cached matte exclusion, and scoped .35h horizon in all four meadow seasons. This replaces ADR-0023's single source and procedural N. Other biomes keep the .26h default; all other motion/load/KST contracts remain.

[ROUND-23](rounds/ROUND-23-meadow-sun-foreground.md): meadow ground scale follows height, with same-direction blended tile margins instead of horizontal stretching/mirror folds. Meadow N groups are staggered at the lower corners. Meadow opts into visible daytime sun on an absolute 0–90° altitude scale, clipped to its actual horizon and behind clouds; existing date calculation is authoritative. This replaces meadow's fixed sun placement, not other biome sky contracts.

[ROUND-27](rounds/ROUND-27-sky-cohesion.md): ten cloud genera retain three source forms each, now composed into coherent weather-family banks with shared transit and 16-bank/10MiB cache; clear skies remain sparse. This supersedes R25 independent genus lanes. Stars retain the attributed HYG subset and Seoul/date/KST projection; the galactic-plane glow has richer cached texture and altitude-based moonlight exposure. Default bearing is south; fixture can compare all four bearings. Meadow sun/moon/stars share the same projection height; terrain clipping remains separate. All non-deep sky consumers compose celestial light before clouds. Original sources and earlier history are retained. Meadow matte pixels are excluded at source resolution before rescaling.

The owner authorized [depth P0](../agent/decisions/ADR-0022-world-depth-and-showcase-motion.md). These clauses replace the corresponding motion/composition implications of AMB-09/10/13/27 below, preserving their other requirements:

- WorldScene composes each panel's sky, F/M/N, weather, haze, above-ground objects and light once. `Scene.composed` prevents an outer duplicate pass. Deep remains sealed even during a pan. Mountain ① is F; nearer terrain and objects share M. Open sea now shares horizon 0.26h.
- All decorative simulation is stationary outside showcase or under reduced motion. Late-art dt=0 retries and minute-level KST refresh are allowed. Mobile `MOBILE_QUERY` prevents engine creation and scene/art requests; CSS hiding alone is insufficient.
- `world/depth.ts` owns bounded pointer offsets and analytic tau. Missing pointer means centered; touch does not drive parallax. Inverse M coordinates govern interaction. Pan remains 620ms with parallax fading to zero.
- Existing auto load and manual preferences remain authoritative. Temporary full/lite/still tiers observe raw RAF before intentional frame skipping, exclude loading/resize warmup, and recover after sustained good samples. Still recovery uses at most 90 raw RAF callbacks, six stationary paint samples and a 30s cooldown; no probes while hidden/mobile/reduced/outside showcase.
- Padded cached source layers use allocation caps before baking and bounded LRU. No runtime blur. `depth-check.mjs` and `depth-perf.mjs` record actual checks and timings; reported bytes are canvas estimates, not total GPU memory.

## World, camera and time

Ground detail policy (owner correction, 2026-09-11): preserve existing distant ground without additional downsampling. R29 replaces R28 mip degradation with new seasonal near-detail artwork, blended only below the source halfway point (smoothstep .5→.85). Original far pixels, geometry, aspect ratio, adaptive cache budgets and stationary/mobile/deep gates remain intact. One 1536×1024 composite adds 6 MiB per active meadow backdrop; no per-frame composition. See [ROUND-29](rounds/ROUND-29-near-ground-detail.md).

R30 supersedes R28 rigid-bank deformation/moisture: each cloud has its own size-dependent life and bounded local drift, with three curved soft fragments that deform and separate individually. Small clouds dissolve faster; overcast retains broad cores. Full enables fragment deformation, lite omits it; all motion still follows engine stationary gates. Cache: at most 20 cloud forms / 16 MiB. This remains artistic behavior, not a forecast. Night lighting ramps use continuous masks rather than overlapping eight-band strips. See [ROUND-30](rounds/ROUND-30-cloud-parts-night-seams.md).

Cloud style exception (owner, 2026-09-11): clouds use smooth resampling and continuous cached shading, including opaque interiors and detached wisps. Do not restore hard pixel steps to preserve the general pixel-art style. Terrain, stars and other entities retain their existing rendering. Evidence: ROUND-27, `world/cloud-field.ts`.

- AMB-01: **계절 배경·바이옴** — Studio and viewer mount the shared AmbientLayer. Default world entry/calendar background is meadow; showcase navigates the biome graph and exit returns to meadow. Water belongs to pond/coast/sea scenes. Do not mount retired WaterTide or introduce a second background system. Evidence: `world/world-scene.ts`, `scenes/biome-loaders.ts`, `ambient-layer.tsx`.
- AMB-02: **보고 있는 달·KST 계절** — Seasons follow the viewed calendar month, not today's season. Special-day overrides use actual KST date. World day uses today for current month, last day for past month, first day for future month. Use world time/date services instead of duplicating rules. 월 경계 검수: 12–2월 겨울, 3–5월 봄, 6–8월 여름, 9–11월 가을(`components/shared/ambient/registry.ts:seasonOfMonth`). 달을 바꾼 프레임과 특수일의 실제 KST 날짜 우선순위를 따로 확인한다.
- AMB-03: **배경 감상·카메라 이동** — Scene loading is lazy; ordinary camera transitions step/draw through WorldScene. Preserve graph directions, folded sea neighbors and current input methods (arrows, WASD, swipe, edge controls, minimap). A forced season preserves current biome. Evidence: `world/biomes.ts`, `world/world-scene.ts`, showcase. 카메라 이동 계약은 620ms(0.62초), ease-out-quint, 오버슈트 없음이다. `components/shared/ambient/world/world-scene.ts:PAN_DUR`, `easeOutQuint`가 숫자 정본이며 reduced motion은 즉시 도착 예외다. 이 소유자 확정 움직임을 바꾸려면 소유자 판단이 필요하다.
- AMB-04: **3/4 카메라·축척·지평선** — Use `world/scale.ts SIZE` and `world/view.ts` helpers. Flat ground/water marks are vertically squashed before rotation; standing objects use depth scaling and ground-contact anchors. Ground objects must remain below horizon. Place horizon-bound scenery by distance with `aboveHz`, ground objects by `groundYAt`, and scale amplitudes with `groundK`. Do not duplicate numeric camera constants. 소유자 확정값: `GROUND_SQUASH=0.7`, `HORIZON_V=0.26`, `DEPTH_FAR=0.6`, 지평선→하단 `depthScale=0.60→1.00`을 0.05 단위로 양자화한다(`world/view.ts`의 해당 심볼). 변경에는 소유자 판단이 필요하며 오래된 주석의 0.8/0.12로 되돌리지 않는다. 크기비 최대:최소 ≤12는 **같은 엔티티의 변형끼리만** 적용한다(2026-09-09 작업 중 소유자 범위 명확화). 서로 다른 엔티티인 나무와 꽃잎 등을 비교해 SIZE를 바꾸지 않는다. 숫자 정본은 `world/scale.ts:SIZE`와 `TILE`이며 이번 범위 명확화로 코드 값을 변경하지 않는다.
- AMB-05: **지평선·산 능선** — The shared horizon supports camera continuity. Mountains use their own layered grammar; do not revive the superseded blanket “all peaks below horizon” instruction. Evidence: [MOUNTAIN_DEPTH_RULES](MOUNTAIN_DEPTH_RULES.md), [ROUND-07](rounds/ROUND-07.md).
- AMB-06: **깊은 바다·심해 예외** — Deep sea is side-on underwater space: no horizon/seafloor, ground squash or standing-object depthScale. Vertical position means depth; per-creature z means distance. It is sealed against season/weather engine passes, but the scene reads time of day for shafts, upper-water brightness and glow. Do not restore “clock has no effect.” Evidence: `scenes/deep.ts`, `scripts/ambient-qa/selftest.mjs`, ADR-0017 deep-sea amendment.
- AMB-07: **세계 시간대·날씨** — Six named time bands are control points of continuous solar-altitude time; use `world/sun.ts`, `time.ts`, `lightAt`. Weather uses seeded calendar/date segments with minimum segment length and previous weather for after-rain conditions, not a real-weather API or old morning/afternoon-only table. A forced band remains a QA/control interface. 세계 시간대·날씨 강제 설정은 developer 전용·session 범위이며 소유자/시청자에게 임의로 확대하지 않는다.
- AMB-08: **달의 흔적·연대기 철회** — World traces are deterministic by calendar slug/year/month. Current traces exclude the retired debut-tree/acorn chronicle; do not restore old treeChain/species instructions from a plan. 개인의 방문·흔적 기록을 추가할 때에는 기기 localStorage에 분리하고 공유 월별 세계의 결정적 결과/DB에 합치지 않는다. 현재 실제 방문 기록은 `showcase.tsx:SEEN_KEY`에서 확인하며 개인 발자국 영속 저장이 구현됐다고 주장하지 않는다. Use `world/traces.ts`; prior narrative lives in [debut-tree archive](../ux/ambient-debut-tree-archive.md).

## Rendering, performance and geometry

- AMB-09: **연속 여력·캐시 굽기** — Bake reusable ground/sprites once per required state and draw cached assets per frame. Continuous load changes gradually from actual frame gaps; keep a visible floor and avoid popping entities. Do not change DPR during a mount or add nondeterministic ground rebakes.
- AMB-10: **배경 효과·가볍게** — Soft effects use lower-resolution layers; sprite/ground marks use cached drawImage. Stop loops while hidden/off/paused. Graphics lite reduces cost while preserving recognizable seasons; soft/off may hide the background. Read existing load/gfx policy rather than adding independent quality gates. 기기 판정은 소프트웨어 렌더러 또는 코어 ≤2이면 soft, 나쁜 방문 두 번 연속일 때만 lite라는 조건을 유지한다(`lib/ui/gfx.ts:softwareRenderer`, `probeGfx`). 한 번의 나쁜 표본으로 계절 배경을 숨기지 않는다. 눈 편한 테마는 현재 모든 등급에서 토큰 팔레트이며 옛 루트 filter 유지 지시는 철회됐다.
- AMB-11: **줌 보정·캔버스 실측** — Measure canvas layout dimensions and compensate pointer/screen measurements for studio zoom. Canvas backing resolution must follow its actual rendered scale. Reason: viewport dimensions inside zoom previously left uncovered background and misplaced input.
- AMB-12: **계절 배경 켜기·흐리게·끄기** — Ambient preference is on/dim/off, default off, stored under `vic.ambient`. One AmbientModeSegment/ViewerAmbientControl serves settings and viewer/studio controls. Dim lowers background opacity/work rate, temporarily lifted in showcase. Motion off renders a still frame rather than hiding the canvas; see [UI rules](../ux/UI_RULES.md). 현행 흐리게는 `.gs-season`의 `--amb-op:0.5`와 엔진 절반 프레임 처리이며 감상 중에는 걷어낸다(`app/ambient.css`의 `--amb-op`, `scene-engine.ts:readDim`/`mountScene`). 옛 0.28은 2026-09-05 소유자가 끄기처럼 보인다고 반려해 대체한 값이다. `.gs-tide`에 남은 0.28을 현행 캔버스 값으로 복원하지 않는다. 이 확정 시각 기준 변경은 소유자 판단이 필요하다.
- AMB-13: **정지 화면·아트 도착** — Fresh still frames require dt=0 scene initialization and bounded retries for late assets. Every consuming scene must rebake on ArtSet version change even without resize. Reason: load completion after first bake otherwise leaves permanent procedural stand-ins.
- AMB-14: **눈 편한 테마·집중 모드** — Keep background separate from eye-comfort palette/filter and text. Heavy media/modal pauses are reference counted through `lib/ui/ambient-pause.ts`; settings stay live. Empty rail/topbar areas must not intercept background input. 계절 배경이 켜진 투명 상단바는 sticky로 셀 위에 겹치지 않고 스크롤되어 사라져야 한다. 편집·끌기 중 집중 모드는 배경만 현행 opacity 0.5로 줄이고 글자/달력의 filter·opacity·blend는 바꾸지 않는다. `.gs-season` 입장 애니메이션은 `backwards`, 끝값은 `var(--amb-op)`여야 한다. `both` 또는 고정 끝값 1로 흐리게를 덮지 않는지 월 전환 전후 computed opacity로 확인한다(`app/ambient.css`의 `gs-season-in`, `--amb-op`).
- AMB-15: **물가·지면 판정** — Bounds, contact, drawing, clipping and pointer interaction share the same shore/ground functions. A curved drawn shore with constant collision bounds allows animals through land. Avoid closure-top constants derived from initial w/h=0; compute from current dimensions. Evidence: [ROUND-17](rounds/ROUND-17.md).
- AMB-16: **앞뒤 가림·안개** — Standing objects and creatures need one y-sorted order where they occlude one another. Use spacing/claimSpot contracts and contact shadows instead of blanket alpha fades. Fog for standing objects applies by ground/depth context uniformly through the object; do not erase its upper half with a screen-height stripe. Evidence: [ROUND-12](rounds/ROUND-12.md), [ROUND-16](rounds/ROUND-16.md).
- AMB-17: **빛·날씨·바람의 실제 반영** — Light/weather must reach their scene consumers: shadows, water, vegetation, particles and contact. A shared field unused by scenes is not completed behavior. Wind magnitude and direction are shared; do not roll contradictory direction in each consumer. Verify actual rendered pixels, not merely assigned values.

## Creatures and sky

- AMB-18: **물고기·곤충·동물 도감** — `world/codex.ts` is the species source for art slots, spawn conditions and catalogue views. Species carry habitat/month/time/weather/after-rain conditions, rarity, size and generation wave. Never restore a second species list. Impossible conditions must fail the catalogue contract tests. 생성 차수 `wave`는 1·2·3이며 현재 종 수와 명단은 `world/codex.ts:CODEX`/`CodexEntry`에서 계산한다. 닌텐도에서 참고하는 것은 도감 형식과 형태 발상뿐이며 종 목록·수치·설명 문구·이미지를 복제하지 않는다.
- AMB-19: **생물 카메라·우리 그림** — Original pixel art is the creature target; Noto/licensed silhouettes remain temporary stand-ins until delivery. Preserve license notices. Standing creatures use high 3/4 placement, ordinary swimming fish use top-view underwater silhouettes; deep species use side view. Apply slot-specific camera exceptions from codex/manifest. 짱뚱어(`fish-mudskipper`)처럼 뻘 위에 서는 물고기는 codex의 `upright` 예외다. fish라는 범주만 보고 모두 shadow로 덮지 않는다.
- AMB-20: **물고기 그림자·오리 수면선** — Side-view sprites use drawFacing (flip plus limited pitch); never rotate the belly upright/downward based on full heading. Flat/top-view sprites use drawSprite with its forward-axis convention. Ducks remain upright flip-only and are visibly submerged below their state-dependent waterline, with contact shadow below.
- AMB-21: **동물 행동·위협·눈 토끼** — Creature behavior is grounded in ethology and must expose debug counters. Keep the shared `threat()` approach-rate/loom model, species-specific reactions and gradual edge arrivals/departures. Existing fish schooling/startle/feeding, rabbit vigilance/flee, squirrel cache/deception and insect behaviors are in their scene functions; code comments provide sources. New behavior must provide the same evidence/debug hooks.
- AMB-22: **희귀도·스폰 감독** — Respect the SpawnDirector rarity/cap/cooldown rules; legendary events use the established probability and session cap. Do not generate impossible catalogue encounters or concurrent events outside the director's contract. 5등급(`world/rarity.ts:TIER_ORDER`), 전설 세션당 최대 1회, 드묾 이상 사건 동시 1개를 유지한다. 가중치·자비 타이머·쿨다운 수치는 `SpawnDirector`와 `TIER_WEIGHT`가 소유하고, 실제 eligible 조건과 debug 기록을 함께 확인한다.
- AMB-23: **하늘 아트·달 위상·희귀 사건** — [ROUND-24](rounds/ROUND-24-sky-art-and-moon.md) replaces the sky ArtSet with one versioned SkyAtlas; other art keeps ArtSet. Cache keys include art version. Moon phases use the existing world date and fractional KST hour with the attributed SunCalc approximation (`world/moon.ts`); a round surface receives a continuous illumination mask instead of eight images. Rare sky events remain pure functions of seed and event index, not mutable shared-module state; two transitioning biomes must not advance events twice. Evidence: `world/sky-events.ts` and its unit tests.
- AMB-24: **혜성·별똥별 진행 방향** — Sprite heading is recorded asset metadata. Compose rotate/flip via the existing aimSprite convention and test its actual transform order. Comet identity comes from traversal behavior, not arbitrary giant scale. Developer force controls repeat for inspection while retaining actual night/weather eligibility.

## QA contract

- AMB-29: **바이옴 시각 QA·입구 묶음 예산** — Same build, URL, seed and fixture parameters must yield the same frame; equivalent advance subdivisions must agree. Test sealed weather behavior separately from time-of-day behavior.
Use canvas capture rather than an element screenshot that includes moving HTML controls. Review before/after sheets visually as well as quantitative probes; a probe using the same broken geometry helper can pass with broken pixels.
검토는 독립 read-only 에이전트 3개(art-mood / spatial-ecology / motion-director)가 병렬로 하고, 메인 세션이 중복 제거·등급 정렬·통합 판단한다. 수정은 P0 전부와 그 밖의 입구 묶음 최대 4개/라운드이며, 각 묶음의 예상 영향 시나리오를 수정 전에 적는다. before 캡처 없이 고치지 않고 검토 중 before 서버 빌드를 바꾸지 않는다. before/after는 같은 seed·시나리오·fixture 조건으로 비교하고 빌드 식별자를 각각 기록한다(결정성 재실행의 같은 빌드 조건과 코드 수정 전후의 빌드 쌍을 혼동하지 않는다). 현재 절차 정본은 [VISUAL_QA_PROTOCOL](VISUAL_QA_PROTOCOL.md) §3.3/3.5/6이다.

Rules are [VISUAL_DIRECTION](VISUAL_DIRECTION.md), matching biome/time/terrain grammars and [VISUAL_QA_PROTOCOL](VISUAL_QA_PROTOCOL.md). Current candidates are [QA_PROGRESS](QA_PROGRESS.md); completed round instructions never supply the next task.

## 원문에서 복원한 측정 계약

- AMB-25: **결정적 캡처·심해 날씨 봉인 검수** — 같은 빌드·URL·시드·크기·load·pointer·시간을 고정한 캔버스를 비교한다. 같은 URL 재방문, `advance(1000) == advance(250)×4 == URL t=1000`, 얼린 뒤 실제 시간이 흘러도 t/픽셀 불변이 계약이다. 심해는 고정 조건에서 새벽·아침·점심·노을·저녁·밤 6띠 × 맑음·흐림·비·안개·바람 5날씨를 캡처해 **각 띠의 5개 해시가 동일하고 전체 고유 해시가 정확히 6개**인지 확인한다. 날씨 봉인과 시간대 반응을 분리한다. `scripts/ambient-qa/selftest.mjs`의 현재 smoke는 다른 띠 1개와 다른 날씨 1개만 비교하므로 smoke PASS를 전체 30장 검증 완료로 보고하지 않는다. 수면 빛줄기·상단 밝기·발광은 `scenes/deep.ts:createDeep`이 소유하며 옛 “시계 영향 없음”과 밤 발광 ×1.5는 현행 기준이 아니다. 현재 밤 발광 ×2.0·반경 ×1.4는 createDeep의 라운드11 대체값으로 확인한다.
- AMB-26: **계절 상호작용·생물 행동의 수치 조회** — 봄 나비/무당벌레/벌/민들레, 여름 오리/물고기/튜브, 가을 낙엽/도토리/다람쥐, 겨울 발자국/눈가루/눈 토끼의 조건·반응·debug를 해당 `createSpring`/`createSummer`/`createAutumn`/`createWinter`에서 확인한다. 도토리 활성 상한 6(`scenes/autumn.ts:ACORN_MAX`, `capAcorns`), 봄 풀 흔들림 12줄(`scenes/spring.ts:ROWS`), 물고기 2–14×면적의 목표와 큰 물고기 load 0.6/0.9 경계(`scenes/summer.ts:fishTarget`, `bigTarget`), 놀람 전염 거리 90px·클릭 놀람 거리 140px·먹이 대기 1초(`createSummer`)는 코드 소유 측정값이다. fishTarget의 현재 load<0.12일 때 0인 조건도 함께 읽는다. 거리 비교는 캔버스 좌표·줌 보정 후 수행하고 debug로 놀람/먹이 반응을 구별한다. 숫자만 맞추고 실제 행동을 보지 않은 검수는 완료가 아니다.
