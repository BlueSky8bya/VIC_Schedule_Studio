Forest opts into fogBeforeAirborne: weather ground fog is drawn before rooted objects, and final light pass skips duplicate fog. Silhouette holes and root transforms remain. Small material also receives matching mist so it cannot float sharply above fog. Other biomes retain existing ordering.

Mist masks are layout-cached at half tree-detail resolution. A fixed neutral color is graded by the shared light pass; changing time/weather only changes opacity, without rebaking every tree mask per color step. Masks plus detail stay inside existing 8/3/1.5 MiB full/lite/still budgets. Nearby samples receive priority over distant foliage. Winter contact masks extend farther across the atlas snow skirt to blend the separate oval into the actual floor.
